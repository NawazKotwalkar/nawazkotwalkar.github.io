~/projects/health-insurance-qa-bot ● live

Health Insurance AI QA Bot

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation chatbot that answers health insurance policy questions strictly from source PDF documents — eliminating LLM hallucination and solving the "pregnancy vs. maternity" vocabulary-mismatch problem that breaks keyword search.

  • Three-phase pipeline: PDF chunking/ingestion → Gemini vector embeddings into ChromaDB → context-grounded generation with cited source pages
  • Answers grounded strictly in uploaded policy documents, not general LLM knowledge
  • Custom mobile-responsive Streamlit UI with a glassmorphism design
